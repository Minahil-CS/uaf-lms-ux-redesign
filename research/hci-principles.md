# HCI Principles Applied
- **Visibility of system status** - loading and progress (U05, U06)
- **Match between system and real world** - clear labels (U03)
- **Recognition rather than recall** - organized courses/topics (U02, U04)
- **Consistency and standards** - consistent navigation and design system
- **Aesthetic and minimalist design** - remove clutter (U01, U08, U09, U12)
- **Accessibility / readability** - larger text and controls (U10)
